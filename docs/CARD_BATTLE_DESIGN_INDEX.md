## Current direction

- Single-player only. No PvP, co-op, player trading, guilds, shared raids or shared economy.
- Discord Bot first; Components V2 is the primary UI.
- Three characters per team, with personal card identities and decks.
- Slay the Spire-inspired Roguelike Expeditions.
- Persona-inspired weakness exploitation, Openings and Relay.
- Balatro-inspired card Patterns and rule-changing Relics.
- Pokétwo / card-bot-inspired personal discovery and collection, without a public claim race or external franchise IP.

## Approval boundary

The values in the gameplay document are prototype hypotheses, not final balance commitments. The combat engine must be prototyped and playtested before finalizing AP costs, draw rules, Pattern value, Relay limits, Relic slots, run length or permanent progression.

No merge, canonical replacement or change to readme.md/main is implied by these documents.
