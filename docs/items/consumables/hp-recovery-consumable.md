## Known direction

HP-restoring consumables have been discussed as a possible recovery option in battle and during Crypt runs. No exact Adventure Time item has been selected, and no final potion name, icon or item artwork is approved.

## Gameplay fields still open

- Flat or percentage healing amount.
- Target: self, one ally or team.
- Whether it can revive a KO character.
- Use restrictions per turn/room/run.
- Whether healing is modified by Rune of Restoration.
- Shop price, drop source and rarity.
- Stack size, sale/trade behavior and inventory limit.

## Player-facing text draft

A recovery item that restores HP. The amount and valid targets are shown in its final item details.

## Implementation boundary

Do not create a named potion, arbitrary healing percentage or drop rate until approved. See [Rune of Restoration](../runes/rune-of-restoration.md) for the separate passive equipment effect.
