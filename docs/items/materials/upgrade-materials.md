# Upgrade Materials

**Category record** · **Status:** category only; no named material approved

## Purpose

Upgrade materials are a possible reward/input category for improving equipment or other progression features. The exact upgradeable systems and material identities are not finalized.

## Known direction

Upgrade Materials may appear as Auto Hunt rewards. No exact item names, icons, rarity, tiers, quantities or enemy sources have been selected.

## Fields required for each future material

- Exact name and stable item key.
- Material family and visual identity.
- Which upgrade operation consumes it.
- Amount required per upgrade level.
- Whether it is universal or item-specific.
- Acquisition sources and drop rates.
- Stack limit, sale/trade rules and inventory behavior.

## Player-facing presentation

The detail panel must show what the material is used for and which upgrades require it. Do not display a vague "upgrade material" as if it were a final named item.

## Open decisions

Upgradeable slots, level caps, material tiers, Gold costs, dismantling/refund rules and recipes.
