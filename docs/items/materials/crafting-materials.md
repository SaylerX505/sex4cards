# Crafting Materials

**Category record** · **Status:** category only; no named material or recipe approved

## Purpose

Crafting Materials are a potential reward category for a future crafting system. The crafting feature itself, its recipes and its output catalogue are not approved.

## Known direction

Crafting Materials have been mentioned as possible Auto Hunt rewards. No specific material name, source, icon, recipe or conversion rate has been selected.

## Fields required for each future material

- Exact name and item identity.
- Source-backed identity if it is a named Adventure Time object.
- Appearance and icon specification.
- Recipe(s) that consume it.
- Required quantity and additional costs.
- Acquisition sources and drop rates.
- Stack, sale and trading behavior.

## Implementation boundary

Do not create invented Adventure Time objects or a recipe system solely to give these materials a use. Approve the crafting loop and individual material list first.
