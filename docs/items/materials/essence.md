Essence has appeared as a possible reward category. Its purpose has not been defined. It may not be assumed to be currency, crafting material, upgrade material or character ascension resource until the progression loop is approved.

## Decisions required

- Which systems consume it.
- How players acquire it.
- Whether it is stackable, tradable or capped.
- Icon and visual identity.
- Balance, rates and UI placement.

Do not implement a balance, shop or recipe around Essence until its function is approved.
