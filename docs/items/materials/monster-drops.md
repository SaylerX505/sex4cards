# Monster Drops

**Category record** · **Status:** category only; no enemy-specific drop approved

## Purpose

Monster Drops are items or materials obtained from defeated enemies. They are a potential reward category across encounters and farming activities.

## Known direction

Auto Hunt may award monster drops. The exact enemy roster, drop identities, quantities, rates and guaranteed rewards remain undefined.

## Required entry for every named drop

- Exact item name and enemy/source.
- Whether it is a material, consumable, currency or equipment item.
- Visual identity and icon.
- Drop conditions, chance and quantity.
- Whether the item is used in crafting, upgrades, a quest or a vendor exchange.
- Inventory stack and sale/trade behavior.

## Boundary

A Monster Drop category is not permission to invent monster names or new Adventure Time objects. Each drop must be tied to approved enemy/content research and have its own dossier when named.
