## Source-backed description

The Ax Bass is a family heirloom axe that Marceline converted into a bass guitar. It functions as both a musical instrument and a weapon. Its defining design combines a broad, double-sided axe head with a bass-guitar neck, strings and tuning hardware. The source material introduces it in "Henchman"; its family history and transformation are explored in "It Came from the Nightosphere".

Use the source name **Ax Bass**. The older concept sheet's label "Bass Axe" is not the catalogue name.
