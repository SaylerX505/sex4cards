## Source-backed description

The Demon Blood Sword is a family sword associated with Joshua and Finn. The source material identifies it as being made from the blood of the demon Kee-Oth. It is introduced in "Dad's Dungeon". Its recognizable design is strongly red, with a distinctive cross-like guard, a long grip and a pale spike/tip detail at the pommel.

The sword's demon-blood origin and story events belong to Adventure Time lore. They do not automatically define an in-game damage type, curse, bonus or status effect.

Reference: [Adventure Time Wiki — Demon Blood Sword](https://adventuretime.fandom.com/wiki/Demon_Blood_Sword).
