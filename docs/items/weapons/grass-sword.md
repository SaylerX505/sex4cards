## Source-backed description

The Grass Sword is a sword associated with Finn and the Grassy Wizard. It first appears in the episode "Blade of Grass". Its defining visual identity is a blade formed from grass-like growth, with a plant/serrated silhouette rather than a conventional metal sword.

The source material depicts unusual cursed/bonding behavior and the sword can be worn around the arm before extending into a blade. Those are franchise-lore details, not approved THE HOLLOW REALMS equipment rules.

Reference: [Adventure Time Wiki — Grass Sword](https://adventuretime.fandom.com/wiki/Grass_Sword).
