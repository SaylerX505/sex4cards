## Visual specification

The supplied reference uses a collection of engraved purple and crimson glyphs with a fiery, aggressive identity. Preserve a distinct damage-oriented silhouette and warm red accents. The final icon should be one isolated, legible symbol or a consistent rune tile—not a screenshot crop.

Reference board: [Rune concept overview](../../assets/references/rune-reference-overview.jpg).

## Gameplay specification

The proposed effect is +10% Attack Damage. The meaning of Attack Damage must be defined in the damage formula: whether it affects basic attacks only or also skills, and whether it applies to physical, magical or all damage.

- One Rune per character.
- Automatic passive effect; no battle action.
- Replacing it returns the old Rune to inventory.
- Stacking with buffs and other multipliers: open.
- Rarity, upgrades and acquisition: open.
- Eligible Auto Hunt reward category: Runes, subject to final drop rules.

## Player-facing text draft

**Rune of Fury** — A fierce engraved Rune proposed to increase the wearer's Attack Damage.

## Decisions required

Final percentage, affected damage sources, formula order, duration/stacking behavior, rarity, upgrade path, acquisition and final icon.
