## Visual specification

The supplied reference uses a warm amber/gold group of engraved symbols. The final icon should communicate recovery and restoration through a clean golden material treatment, controlled glow and distinct glyph shape.

Reference board: [Rune details](../../assets/references/rune-reference-details.jpg).

## Gameplay specification

The proposed effect is +12% Healing Effectiveness. Define which healing sources are affected: self-healing, ally-targeted skills, consumables and any end-of-round recovery may not all follow the same rule.

- One Rune per character.
- Passive effect; not a battle action.
- Replacing it returns the old Rune to inventory.
- Healing formula and rounding: open.
- Rarity, upgrades and acquisition: open.
- Runes are a possible Auto Hunt reward category.

## Player-facing text draft

**Rune of Restoration** — A golden Rune proposed to improve the healing received from the wearer's healing effects.

## Decisions required

Final percentage, affected healing sources, formula order, stacking, rarity, upgrade path, acquisition and final icon.
