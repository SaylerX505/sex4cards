The proposed effect is +10% Damage Reduction. The combat formula must define whether the bonus is additive with DEF, multiplicative with other reduction effects, capped, or applied after other modifiers.

- One Rune per character.

- Replacing it returns the old Rune to inventory.
- Damage reduction formula and cap: open.
- Rarity, upgrades and acquisition: open.
- Runes are a possible Auto Hunt reward category.
