## Visual specification

The supplied reference shows a gray stone-like Rune surrounded by a cool green/teal energy aura. Keep the stone texture readable, with a restrained life glow and a strong outer silhouette.

Reference board: [Rune concept overview](../../assets/references/rune-reference-overview.jpg).

## Gameplay specification

The proposed effect is +15% maximum HP. Define whether the bonus also changes current HP on equip/unequip, level-up or replacement; do not assume current HP is automatically refilled.

- One Rune per character.
- Passive effect; not a battle action.
- Replacing it returns the old Rune to inventory.
- HP recalculation and rounding: open.
- Rarity, upgrades and acquisition: open.
- Runes are a possible Auto Hunt reward category.

## Player-facing text draft

**Rune of Vitality** — A life-infused stone Rune proposed to increase the wearer's maximum HP.

## Decisions required

Final percentage, current-HP recalculation behavior, rounding, stacking, rarity, upgrade path, acquisition and final icon.
