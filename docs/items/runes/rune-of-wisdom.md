## Visual specification

The supplied reference uses a circular blue magical sigil with a luminous center and layered circular markings. Maintain a clear blue/cyan identity and a readable ring silhouette at small sizes.

Reference boards: [Rune overview](../../assets/references/rune-reference-overview.jpg) and [Rune details](../../assets/references/rune-reference-details.jpg).

## Gameplay specification

The proposed effect is +15% maximum Mana. Define whether the increase changes current Mana on equip/unequip and how the value rounds. Do not treat this as Mana regeneration: the reference says Max Mana, not regeneration.

- One Rune per character.
- Passive effect; not a battle action.
- Replacing it returns the old Rune to inventory.
- Mana regeneration rules remain separate and slow.
- Rarity, upgrades and acquisition: open.
- Runes are a possible Auto Hunt reward category.

## Player-facing text draft

**Rune of Wisdom** — A blue sigil proposed to increase the wearer's maximum Mana.

## Decisions required

Final percentage, current-Mana recalculation, rounding, stacking, rarity, upgrade path, acquisition and final icon.
